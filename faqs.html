Before You Visit

Etiquette & Answers

Everything you need to know for a comfortable, respectful, and beautiful experience.

What should I expect during my first visit? +

From the moment you arrive, you'll be welcomed into a private, beautifully prepared space. Your session begins with a gentle introduction, allowing you to settle in and share any preferences or boundaries. From there, the experience unfolds at a pace that feels right for you. Everything is unhurried, intentional, and entirely focused on your comfort.

Is Good Girls welcoming to all genders? +

Absolutely. Good Girls is an inclusive space, welcoming both men and women who seek a sophisticated, sensual, and respectful experience. Whoever you are, if you value intention, discretion, and beautiful presentation, you belong here.

How should I prepare for my session? +

Simply arrive as you are. We provide everything you'll need — towels, warm oils, and refreshments. We ask only that you arrive on time to allow yourself to settle into the experience. Avoid heavy meals or alcohol beforehand.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Free rescheduling is available up to 12 hours before your scheduled appointment. Cancellations made less than 12 hours before your appointment may incur a cancellation fee. We understand life happens — just let us know as soon as possible.

How do payments work? +

Payment is required before your session to secure your booking. We accept cash (NZD) and credit/debit cards. All prices include GST where applicable, and receipts are available upon request.

Is my visit completely private? +

Yes. Discretion is at the heart of everything we do. Your visit, your details, and your experience remain entirely confidential. Our West Auckland location is shared only after booking confirmation to ensure your privacy.

Can I request a specific girl? +

Yes, absolutely. When booking, you're welcome to request a specific practitioner. While we always do our best to accommodate requests, availability depends on scheduling. If your preferred girl is unavailable, we'll let you know and help you choose another beautiful experience.

Beautiful experiences are built on mutual respect. Our etiquette guidelines exist to ensure every guest — and every woman — feels safe, valued, and held in the highest regard.

Arriving & Settling In

  • Please arrive on time for your scheduled session.
  • Hygiene matters — please arrive showered and freshly prepared.
  • Silence your phone and leave the outside world behind.
  • Avoid heavy meals, alcohol, or strong fragrances before your session.

During Your Experience

  • Communication is welcome — share what feels good, what doesn't, and what you'd like more of.
  • Trust the pace. Our sessions are intentionally unhurried.
  • Respect the silence when it comes — sometimes the most beautiful moments are wordless.
  • Let yourself be present. This is your time to receive.

Respect & Boundaries

Every woman at Good Girls is a practitioner of sensual touch — not a provider of sexual services. Clear, respectful boundaries are what allow our space to remain beautiful, safe, and sophisticated.

  • Consent flows both ways. Every touch is offered and received with intention.
  • Services not listed in our package must only be performed with explicit consent.
  • Requests for services outside our offerings are not permitted.
Please note: Sexual intercourse is not considered an extra and is not a service we offer. Any attempt to pressure our practitioners into providing this will result in the immediate termination of your session without refund.

Extra Services

Extra services are additional offerings not featured in our standard sensual massage packages and may vary from one masseuse to another. We gladly embrace the opportunity to enhance your experience with these extras.

  • The fees for extras vary between practitioners and pricelists are available on request.
  • Any attempts to request services not included in the massage package will result in immediate termination of your session, without refund.

Leaving Gracefully

  • Take your time. There's no rush to leave the experience.
  • Refreshments and a quiet moment to reorient are always offered.
  • A small thank-you gift is often available for guests returning for repeat visits.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Reach out before you book — we'd love to hear from you.
