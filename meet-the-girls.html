At Good Girls, every experience is carefully curated by Missy a therapist who brings warmth, presence, and a deep understanding of feminine energy to every session. Her approach is intuitive, unhurried, and beautifully considered.

Missy

FOUNDER · PRACTITIONER

Warm, playful and attentive, Missy's style is slow and sensual, with each experience shaped around presence, connection and making you feel completely at ease.
