Etiquette & Boundaries

Things worth knowing

Clear expectations, respectful boundaries, and a commitment to your comfort.

What should I expect during my first visit?

From the moment you arrive, you'll be welcomed into a private, beautifully prepared space. Your session begins with a gentle introduction, allowing you to settle in and share any preferences. From there, the experience unfolds at a pace that feels right for you.

Is Good Girls welcoming to all genders?

Absolutely. Good Girls is an inclusive space, welcoming both men and women who seek a sophisticated, sensual, and respectful experience.

What are the boundaries?

Our experiences are sensual, not sexual. Respect, consent, and discretion are at the heart of everything we do. Any behaviour that crosses these boundaries will result in the immediate termination of the session without refund.

How do I prepare for my session?

Simply arrive as you are. We provide everything you need — towels, warm oils, and refreshments. We ask only that you arrive on time to allow yourself to settle into the experience.

How do payments work?

Payment is required before your session to secure your booking. We accept cash (NZD) and credit/debit cards. All prices include GST where applicable, and receipts are available upon request.
